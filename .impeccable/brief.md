Mode: Operate. Audience: French-speaking amateur athletes, one dedicated session each. ## Direction contract

THESIS: The programme is printed like the poster for your own club gala. Refuses the dark fitness app with one neon accent, progress rings and rounded stat cards.

OWN-WORLD: Affiche fluo. Dayglo paper stocks carry meaning: yellow training, orange cardio, pink nutrition, green coach, white for rest and forms; black ink rail and type; one variable grotesque from extra-condensed to wide; thick 3px printed rules; square corners; standard web controls.

STORY: The visitor answers four steps, sees their programme come off the press, understands what to do today and why, and adjusts it.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Black left rail with wordmark, five nav items and session switcher. Main: date heading, seven-day strip, then a wide yellow session sheet beside a pink breakfast sheet and a green coach sheet. Primary action: the day's check-in on the coach sheet.

FORM: Affiche de gala fluo, position 4 of 7 on the grounded list, seed key a5b43ddd. Signature interaction: the session set as a billed card, each exercise name stretched on the width axis to fill its line, and one niveau slider that re-sets every load and set count live. Motion grammar: sheets wipe in top to bottom once after generation, like coming off a press.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
