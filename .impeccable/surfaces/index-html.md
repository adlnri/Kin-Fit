# Surface: Kin Fit app (index.html)

Mode: Operate. Audience: French-speaking amateur athletes, one dedicated session each on a shared device, who challenge each other. Job: follow today's training and breakfast, complete daily, weekly and sport challenges (training and nutrition), send and accept challenges between people, collect stickers in an album. User brief (pinned): "plus jolie et ludique avec des défis", challenges "envoyés et partagés entre les personnes", "aussi la nutrition". Direction proposed in chat ("l'album de défis"); the user answered the two feature questions ("les defis pourront être envoyé et partagés challenge entre les personnes", "Aussi la nutrition") but did not explicitly approve the look. To be shown and confirmed. Name: Kin Fit (chosen by the user). User pin, 2026-10-21: "je veux quelque chose de plus beau \"sparks gold and black\"".

Unresolved: real model, real accounts and server sync for sharing (today: same-device sessions plus a share link), product name.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Training as a sticker album you fill with friends. Refuses the dark fitness app with neon rings and the sober coaching dashboard.

OWN-WORLD: Gold and black, with real sparks (user-pinned), premium and not childish, after top sport apps (user, 2026-10-21). Near-black surfaces with hairline borders; metallic gold (#d4af37 base, gradient sheen) for primary actions, selection, ranks and medals; categories as quiet tinted icons and outlines (ember sport, ember-rose cardio, lime nutrition, ice water, lilac friends). Trophies are struck gold medals with engraved line art in a vitrine; locked slots are dark coins with a number. Sora for display, Manrope for text, both self-hosted.

STORY: The visitor sees today's challenges, ticks exercises, logs breakfast and water, earns points and stickers, and dares a friend.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Indigo rail with six destinations; greeting with inbox, streak, points and rank-badge chips; week strip; three daily challenge cards, each showing the sticker it counts toward; session checklist beside breakfast, water and coach cards.

FORM: Album de défis, proposed in chat from the user's brief (no roll this round; look not yet explicitly approved). Signature interaction: a medal minted on screen (blur-to-sharp, brightness flash) with a canvas burst of real sparks: white-hot heads cooling to gold then ember, trails, flicker, gravity and branching when a challenge or milestone is won; a repeat win sticks a duplicate. Motion: one stick animation, short pops on state change.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
